# 🍄 Pink Oyster Growing Steps

Species: Pink Oyster (*Pleurotus djamor*)
Spawn: 1 lb colonized grain spawn bag
Substrate: Straw (chopped), 3x zip bags (33×39cm)

---

## Day 0 — Inoculation

1. Chop straw to 5-10cm lengths
2. Pasteurize in 75°C water for 1-1.5 hours
3. Drain, cool to 25°C — squeeze test: barely dripping
4. Divide grain spawn equally across 3 zip bags, mix into straw
5. Squeeze out air, zip shut
6. Place on kitchen shelf above appliance (dark, ~27-30°C)

---

## Days 1-14 — Colonization (no Pi, no intervention)

- Check through bag every 2-3 days — do not open
- ✅ White spreading mycelium = good, keep going
- ❌ Green/black anywhere = remove that bag immediately before spores spread
- Goal: fully white throughout the bag

---

## Day ~14 — Transfer to Fruiting Chamber

1. Set up open cubicle with elevated containers (drainage holes facing down)
2. Tear open bags, pack straw loosely into containers
3. Start Pi monitoring (SHT31 + camera)
4. Start humidifier, fan, 12h/day light cycle
5. Ceramic heater maintains 24-28°C ambient
6. Heating mat maintains substrate ~25°C

---

## Days 17-21 — Pinning

- Tiny pins appear on straw surface
- Do not mist directly onto pins
- Maintain 80-85% RH and continuous fresh airflow
- Check Pi dashboard daily

---

## Days 21-25 — Harvest

- Harvest when veil under cap just starts to tear — do not wait
- Twist and pull entire cluster at once
- Pink oyster drops spores quickly once mature — harvest early

---

## Between Flushes

1. Remove any leftover stumps from containers
2. Rest substrate 5-7 days
3. Optional: cold water soak for 12h to rehydrate straw
4. Resume humidifier and monitoring
5. Expect 2-3 flushes before straw is spent

---

## Target Parameters (Pi Monitored)

| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Colonization temp (ambient) | 27-30°C |
| Fruiting temp (ambient) | 24-28°C |
| Substrate temp | ~25°C |
| Relative humidity (fruiting) | 80-85% |
| Light cycle | 12h on / 12h off |
